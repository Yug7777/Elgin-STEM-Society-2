Got Questions?

Is there any fee to join?

No, joining the Elgin STEM Society is 100% free for all students!

Do I need prior STEM or coding experience?

Not at all! Everyone is welcome, whether you are completely new or already building cool projects.

How often do meetings take place?

We meet bi-weekly. Check back here or sign up for exact dates and times!

Can I both learn and volunteer?

Yes! You can sign up as a student participant and also volunteer to lead or assist with activities.

What age or grade levels can join?

Our club is open to students in high school or below who live in Elgin or surrounding communities.

What if I can't attend every meeting?

That is totally okay! Come when you can, though regular attendance helps you get the most out of projects.

How do I get started?

Simply click on the registration links in the "Join Us" section to fill out our quick sign-up form!
